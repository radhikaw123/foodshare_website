Getting Started

To create an account, tap the "Sign Up" button on the login screen. Enter your email, create a password, and follow the verification steps. Once verified, you can set up your profile with your name, photo, and preferences.

Go to your Profile page from the dashboard. Add a profile picture, write a short bio, set your location, and customize your privacy settings. A complete profile helps build trust with the community.

Required: Email and password. Optional: You can control what information is visible through Privacy Settings. We recommend sharing your name and general location for better connections with other users.

Donations & Sharing

Tap the "+" button or "New Donation" from the dashboard. Add photos of the food, describe what you're sharing, specify quantity, expiry date, and pickup location. Then publish to make it visible to others.

Yes! Go to "My Donations" and select the post you want to edit or delete. You can update details, photos, or remove it completely if the food is no longer available.

You can share cooked meals, fresh produce, packaged foods, baked goods, or prepared dishes. Ensure food is fresh, properly stored, and safe for consumption. Do not share items that are expired or unsafe.

📍

Pickup & Delivery

Tap "Nearby Donations" to see available food in your area. The map shows locations, and you can filter by distance or food type. Tap any donation to see details and contact the donor.

Find a donation you're interested in, tap on it, and click "Request Pickup" or "Contact Donor". Communicate the time and location with the donor. Once arranged, tap "Confirm Pickup" when you collect the food.

Delivery availability depends on your location and donor preferences. Some donors may offer delivery for nearby pickups. Check the donation details to see if delivery is available.

Account & Safety

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?". Enter your email, and we'll send you a password reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to create a new password.

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. You control what information is visible through Privacy Settings. We never share your data with third parties without permission.

Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Blocking & Reports to report inappropriate content or users. Provide details about the issue, and our team will review and take action within 24-48 hours.

Ratings & Reviews

After each transaction, you can rate the other user (1-5 stars) and leave feedback. These ratings help build trust in the community. Your rating is displayed on your profile.

You can edit or delete your review within 30 days of posting it. Go to the donation details and find your review to edit it. After 30 days, it cannot be modified.
