FoodShare

Connecting Communities Through Food

A platform dedicated to reducing food waste and feeding hungry communities by connecting food donors with those in need.

🎯

Our Mission

At FoodShare, we believe that no food should go to waste and no one should go hungry. We're building a community-driven platform that makes it easy for individuals, restaurants, and organizations to share surplus food with people who need it.

Our mission is to create a more sustainable and compassionate world where food abundance is shared, not wasted.

✨

Our Vision

We envision a world where:

  • Food waste is minimized and food is shared equitably
  • Communities are strengthened through mutual support
  • Everyone has access to nutritious food
  • Sustainability and social responsibility are prioritized

Our Team

FoodShare was founded by a passionate team of social entrepreneurs, developers, and community advocates who believe in the power of technology to solve social problems. We're driven by the mission to end hunger and reduce food waste.

Our team is composed of volunteers and full-time staff members from diverse backgrounds, all united by the common goal of making a positive impact in their communities.
